Strategic positioning insight
The Hive (same block) prices lattes at $5.50 and drip at $4.00 — but their espresso is a budget $2.25, signaling they're not chasing the craft tier. Golden Hour is your true craft peer: lattes at $6, specialty at $7-8. Puente's sweet spot is between them — craft quality matching Golden Hour, community feel to rival The Hive, priced to reflect both. Recommended moves: espresso $4.00, cortado $4.50, latte 12oz $6.00, add a signature latte at $7-7.50. On cold brew, The Hive's $4.50+ gives you room to hold at $5.25 and lead on quality.
